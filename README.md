
# Bitcoin Underdog

**Real-life experiences. Practical learning.**

A **free, fully open-source** Bitcoin course that teaches through real stories from around the world and hands-on interactive tools, so you learn by doing, not just by reading or watching.

> **Free forever to learn. Open for anyone to improve.** No paywalls, no sign-ups required, no prior Bitcoin or programming knowledge needed.

---

## Why Bitcoin Underdog?

Most Bitcoin education is either dense and technical or abstract and detached from real life. Bitcoin Underdog connects the two:

- **Stories** show why Bitcoin matters to real people in different countries and circumstances.
- **Concepts** explain the ideas behind each story in clear, beginner-friendly language.
- **Interactive practice** lets you try it yourself in a safe, simulated environment.

For example, while learning about wallets you can open a demo wallet and practise sending and receiving simulated transactions, with no real money at risk.

## How it works

Every lesson follows the same simple loop:

| Step | What you do |
|------|-------------|
| **1. Hear the story** | Meet someone whose life intersects with Bitcoin and see the challenge or opportunity they faced. |
| **2. Learn the concept** | Understand the Bitcoin principle that explains their story. |
| **3. Practise it** | Open the linked interactive tool and try the idea in a guided, risk-free setting. |

Interactive tools appear throughout the course, not as an optional extra at the end. The tool you use depends on the topic being taught.

> **Demo tools are simulations.** Unless a tool explicitly says otherwise, it does not send real Bitcoin or touch the live Bitcoin network.

## What you'll learn

The topics may evolve as the course grows, but you can expect to build an understanding of:

- What Bitcoin is and the problems it was designed to address
- How the network and transactions work at a foundational level
- How wallets, keys, addresses, and fees fit together
- How to explore Bitcoin concepts using interactive tools
- How people around the world use and experience Bitcoin
- Wallet safety, privacy, backups, and self-custody
- How to evaluate Bitcoin claims, risks, and trade-offs

## Who is this for?

Curious beginners and anyone who wants a human, hands-on way to learn Bitcoin. No previous experience is required unless a specific module says otherwise. It is also useful for educators and community organisers looking for free, adaptable teaching material.

## Getting started

1. Browse the course materials in this repository.
2. Start with the introductory module (when available).
3. Follow the lessons in order where a sequence is provided.
4. Open the interactive tool linked to each lesson and follow its instructions.
5. Stick to demo or simulated environments unless a lesson clearly explains otherwise.
6. Read the safety notes before trying any wallet or transaction exercise.

> **Note:** Not every module or tool listed in this repository may be available yet. Check the project documentation for what's currently released.

## Safety and disclaimer

Bitcoin Underdog is an educational project. Everything here is for general information and learning only, and is **not** financial, investment, legal, or tax advice.

Bitcoin carries real risks, including price volatility, irreversible transactions, loss of access to funds, scams, and software or security failures. Please keep these rules in mind:

- **Never enter a real wallet's recovery phrase or private keys into any educational tool.**
- Use demo or test environments for practice wherever possible.
- Check each tool's instructions to see whether it uses simulated data, a test network, or the live network.
- Carefully verify addresses and transaction details before acting on a live network.
- Real-life stories illustrate individual experiences. They are not guarantees of results. Think critically and make your own decisions.
